Siege saves matches as binary `.rec` files. Parsing is built on
[r6-dissect](https://github.com/redraskal/r6-dissect), an open-source Go parser, which I patched for the
fields we needed. My Python layer batch-processes folders of replays and turns them into per-player and
per-round stats our team could actually compare.
