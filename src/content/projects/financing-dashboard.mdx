A personal tool that runs all day on my server. Scheduled jobs pull prices from Finnhub, TwelveData and
CoinGecko, news from RSS feeds, insider transactions and Reddit mentions, then compute technical
indicators and a sentiment score per ticker. When a signal fires I get an email with the reasoning.

To keep it honest, every signal is logged with its outcome, paper trades
are placed automatically, and walk-forward backtests check whether a rule still works on data it wasn't
tuned on.

<p class="note">Private: it's behind a password because it's my own watchlist.</p>
