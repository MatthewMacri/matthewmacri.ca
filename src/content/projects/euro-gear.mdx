Euro Gear is a precision manufacturing company. I rebuilt their website in Next.js and took over
everything around it: hosting, deploys, performance and SEO.

The first thing a visitor picks is their region (Canada, the US or international) so they get the right
sales office and phone number. Most of the ongoing work is invisible: page speed, uptime, and making sure
the site shows up for the parts people search for.
