Most dating apps reward whoever sends the most likes, so a few profiles get flooded and everyone else
goes unseen. Likewise is my attempt at the opposite: a design where attention is shared fairly instead of
won by volume.

## The idea

- **Reciprocity first.** Likes only go to people who have room to answer them, and the deck is ranked by
  the chance you *both* say yes — not by who paid for reach.
- **Everyone gets seen, no one gets flooded.** Match and chat "rooms" have a capacity, so conversations
  get a real chance instead of piling up unanswered.
- **Safety and fairness built in, not bolted on.** Selfie verification, reporting with a real review
  queue, pause mode, and clear, published ranking rules — designed against the actual store, privacy and
  online-safety requirements (Apple, Google, EU DSA, Québec Law 25).

## How it's built

An Expo / React Native app for iOS and Android on a Fastify and PostgreSQL backend, in TypeScript. I'm
building the product, the backend and the brand.

## Status

In progress and pre-launch — I'm building the onboarding, the matching logic and the safety tooling
toward a first test. No public build or screenshots yet.
