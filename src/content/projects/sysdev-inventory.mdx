Team capstone for Systems Development at Vanier, built for a company that makes driveline components:
gears, shafts and related equipment. It tracks stock, ties parts and processes to projects, and follows
work through the shop floor, with role-based logins. A GitHub Actions workflow runs the test suite on every push.
