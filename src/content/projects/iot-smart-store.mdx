IoT course project, built in four phases. Customers add items by barcode, RFID tag or manual UPC entry
and pay at a self-checkout screen; the hardware side is C++ on a microcontroller talking to a Python
backend. Staff get a dashboard for stock and sales. Services run in Docker.
